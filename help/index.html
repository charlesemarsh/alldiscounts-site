On the product page

Offers shoppers act on while they choose.

  • Quantity discountsTiered pricing that grows with how many a shopper buys.
  • BundlesA set of products at a bundle price.
  • UpsellsDiscounted add-ons shown next to a product.

In the cart

Rewards that grow the order.

  • Cart goalsA reward once the cart reaches a set amount, with a progress bar.
  • Buy X get YBuying some products makes others free or cheaper.
  • Free giftsGifts added to the cart automatically or chosen from a popup.
  • Shipping discountsFree or cheaper shipping, automatically or with a code.
  • Advanced discount codesOne code that combines product, order and shipping discounts.

Across the store

Sales that change the price everyone sees.

  • Bulk price salesLower prices across many products for a set time, then put them back.

Before and after the visit

Bring shoppers in, and bring buyers back.

  • Magic linksLinks that unlock a discount for people arriving from an email, ad or post.
  • QR codesPrintable codes that open your store with a discount applied.
  • Behaviour triggersOffers that appear based on what a shopper does.
  • Sign-up popupsCollect emails, with a code or gift as the thank-you.
  • Thank-you page offersProducts offered after the order is placed.
  • Checkout cross-sellsAdd-on offers inside checkout, for Shopify Plus stores.
