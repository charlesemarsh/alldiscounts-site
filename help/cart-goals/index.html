Cart goals

A cart goal unlocks a reward once the cart subtotal reaches an amount you choose: money off the order or a shipping discount. A progress bar in the cart shows shoppers how far they have to go.

Set it up

  1. In the app, go to Discount Campaigns and click Create a campaign.
  2. Find Cart goal and click Create.
  3. Enter a Campaign name. Only you see it.
  4. Under Goal, set Unlock when cart subtotal reaches to the amount the cart has to hit.
  5. Under Reward, pick a Reward type: Order discount or Shipping discount.
  6. Choose the Discount value: Percentage or Fixed amount, and for a shipping reward also Free shipping. Enter the percentage or amount.
  7. Optionally write a Storefront message (see below) and decide whether to tick Show progress bar in the cart drawer.
  8. Check Combinations, Customer selection and Schedule, then click Create cart goal.

The goal is an automatic discount, so there’s no code to enter. It goes live as soon as you save, or at the start date if you scheduled it.

What shoppers see

The progress bar shows how much more the shopper needs to spend and what they’ll get, for example “You’re £12.00 away from free shipping”. It updates as items are added, changed or removed. When the cart reaches the goal, the bar says the reward is unlocked.

There are two ways to put the bar in the cart:

  • Cart goal progress app block. In the theme editor, add the Cart goal progress block to your cart page or cart drawer section. Its Show ‘unlocked’ message when goal is reached setting decides whether the bar stays visible once the goal is met.
  • Automatic cart drawer bar. With Show progress bar in the cart drawer ticked, the app adds the bar to your cart drawer itself, even if your theme doesn’t let you add the block there. If it doesn’t appear, or appears in the wrong spot, set its position under Advanced settings > Widget placement > Cart goal bar in the cart drawer.

Both need the app embed turned on in your theme. See Storefront display.

The reward itself is applied in the cart and at checkout. A shipping reward only shows once the shopper reaches the shipping step.

Storefront message

Leave Storefront message (optional) blank to use your default copy, or write your own using these tokens:

TokenReplaced with
{{remaining}}The amount left to spend
{{reward}}The reward, such as “free shipping” or “10% off your order”
{{threshold}}The goal amount

A message you write here is also shown once the goal is reached, so word it so it still makes sense then.

Several goals on one bar

You can run more than one cart goal, for example free shipping at £40 and 10% off at £80. The bar counts down to the next goal the shopper hasn’t reached. Free gifts set to unlock at a cart subtotal can join the same bar: tick Show on the cart goal progress bar on the free gift. See Free gifts.

Change how the bar looks

Go to Customise and click Customize on Cart Goal Widget. You can set:

  • Colours: card background and border, text, the empty track, and the fill before and after the goal is unlocked.
  • Default copy: the In-progress message used by goals with no storefront message, and the Unlocked message.
  • Tiers: used when the bar has more than one tier or a free gift. Choose a Layout (Next reward card, other tiers as chips, Milestones along the bar or Banner with big circles), whether gift tiers show the product image or a gift icon, and the image size.

Good to know

  • The goal is checked against the cart subtotal. Thresholds and fixed amounts are in your store currency and converted for shoppers paying in another currency.
  • New cart goals combine with product, order and shipping discounts by default. If you untick a box under Combinations, another discount can win at checkout instead. If an order reward isn’t actually on the cart, the bar leaves that goal out rather than show it as unlocked.
  • When a scheduled goal ends, it stops showing on the bar.
  • Customer selection is All customers for now; customer segments are marked as coming soon.
