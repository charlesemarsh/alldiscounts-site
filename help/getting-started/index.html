Getting started

Install the app, switch on the theme app embed so it can run on your storefront, and create your first discount.

Install the app

  1. Install All Discounts & Promotions from the Shopify App Store and approve the permissions it asks for.
  2. Shopify opens the app on its Home page. You start on the Free plan, with no card needed. The Free plan works on live stores as well as development stores.

Switch on the theme app embed

The app embed is what runs the app on your storefront. Without it, shoppers won’t see magic links, behaviour triggers or popups, and widgets that the app places for you won’t appear. The app checks your published theme for it.

  1. If the embed has never been added to your published theme, Home shows Finish installing All Discounts & Promotions. Click Open theme editor.
  2. Shopify opens the theme editor with the app embed selected. Turn on Discounts & Promotions if it isn’t on already, then click Save.
  3. Go back to the app and click I’ve saved the embed. Home loads once the app can see the embed.

If the embed is in your theme but switched off, Home shows a banner saying the app embed is turned off, with an Enable app embed button that takes you to the same place.

The embed belongs to a theme. If you publish a different theme later, switch the embed on in that theme too.

Create your first discount

  1. In the app menu, click Discount Campaigns, then Create a campaign.
  2. Pick a campaign type, such as Quantity discount, Bundle, Cart goal or Magic link. Click Learn more on any card to see when to use it, or Create to start.
  3. Give it a name (only you see this), set the discount and the products or customers it covers, and choose start and end dates if you want it to run for a set time.
  4. Click Save.

Each campaign type has its own guide with the details. Some offers, like quantity tiers and bundles, show on the product page through an app block you add in the theme editor; the guide for that type explains where.

Find and manage your campaigns

Every campaign you create, of every type, is listed under Discount Campaigns. Use the All, Active, Scheduled and Expired tabs, filter by type, search by name, or change the sort order. Click View on a row to open and edit that campaign.

Pause, activate or delete

  1. In Discount Campaigns, tick the box next to one or more campaigns.
  2. Click Pause to stop them, Activate to turn them back on, or Delete to remove them.

Pausing keeps the campaign and its settings so you can turn it back on later. Deleting also removes the matching Shopify discount and can’t be undone.

Bulk price editor campaigns work differently because they change your actual product prices. You can’t pause or activate them from the list: open the campaign and click End campaign, which puts the original prices back. They can only be deleted once they’ve ended. See Bulk price sales.

Good to know

  • Home shows your current plan and how many discounted orders you’ve had this period.
  • To tag orders that used one of the app’s discounts, go to Settings and turn on Enable order tagging. You can set your own tag text.
  • You can change the language of the app itself under Settings > Account language. This doesn’t change what shoppers see.
  • Before uninstalling, end any Bulk price editor campaigns so your original prices are restored.
